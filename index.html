I study how flat woven sheets become three-dimensional structures — and how to design the flat pattern that produces a shape you want.

Research vision

My work starts with a physical system, builds mathematics that explains it, pushes that mathematics past the limits it was written for, and then carries the result back into something you can hold. Woven architected materials are where all three steps meet: the geometry is simple enough to write down, rich enough to be hard, and cheap enough to cut and test the same afternoon.

A woven sheet is an unusual kind of material. It has no continuum to speak of — just strips, contacts, and friction — yet it carries load, stores energy in bending, and folds itself into shapes that were never drawn. I want a design language for that: a notation for the pattern, a model fast enough to search over, and a route from a target shape back to the flat sheet that makes it.

Advised by Evgueni T. Filipov and Talia Y. Moore.

Research

Shape-morphing woven architected materials

Flat sheets woven from passive strips and heat-shrinking active strips. Contracting a chosen subset of the weavers puts the sheet in conflict with itself, and it resolves that conflict by leaving the plane. The design question is which strips, how wide, and how spaced.

Reduced-order modeling and inverse design

Full structural simulation of a weave is too slow to search over, so I build reduced-order models that keep the deformation physics that matters and drop the rest. The model is the forward engine; the goal is inverse design over geometric parameters — weaver widths, spacings, and the pattern itself.

Weave notation and simulation tooling

A grammar for writing down woven structures that mix active and passive weavers, with validity rules that reject patterns that cannot be woven, and a renderer that draws them. Behind it, a finite element code for biaxial woven sheets with analytical Hessians and arc-length continuation through instability.

Rotational electrostatic clutches

A device line running alongside the materials work: a rotational electrostatic clutch that engages and releases under low power, with an eye toward compact robotic transmissions.

Code & tools

weave_render
Python renderer for the weave notation. Takes a pattern, checks it against the validity rules, and writes SVG or PDF.
Woven sheet simulator
MATLAB finite element code for biaxial woven sheets: bar, connector, angular and dihedral spring elements, with Newton–Raphson and arc-length solvers.
