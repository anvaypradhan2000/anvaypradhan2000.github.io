Research

Four threads, one question: what design language does a material need when it is made of strips instead of stuff?

Architected materials and discrete mechanical systems

Structures whose behavior comes from how their parts are arranged and how they touch, not from a bulk constitutive law.

Shape-morphing woven sheets

Flat sheets woven from passive and active strips that leave the plane when a chosen subset contracts. The mechanics live in the crossings: contact, friction, and the bending each strip resists.

Ongoing, with E. T. Filipov and T. Y. Moore

A grammar for woven structures

A notation that writes down a weave as a string rather than a picture, covering mixed active and passive weavers, with a rule set that rejects patterns that cannot physically be woven.

Ongoing

Kinematics of interlaced strip networks

What a weave can and cannot do before any material properties are assigned — the mobility and constraint counting that bounds the space of reachable shapes.

Ongoing

Reduced-order modeling and inverse design

Full simulation of a weave is far too slow to search over. These projects build models that keep the physics that matters, drop the rest, and run fast enough to sit inside a design loop.

A reduced-order model for shape-morphing woven surfaces

A low-dimensional description of the morphed shape driven by the weave's geometric parameters, validated against full structural simulation and physical specimens.

Manuscript in preparation

Inverse design over weave geometry

Going backward from a target surface to the flat pattern that produces it, searching over weaver widths, spacings, and which strips are active. The reduced-order model is the forward engine that makes the search affordable.

Ongoing

Structural simulation of biaxial woven sheets

A finite element code built for this class of structure: bar, connector, angular and dihedral spring elements with analytical Hessians, solved through instability with arc-length continuation.

MATLAB, ongoing

Enumerating the design space

Generating weave libraries at scale, with symmetry deduplication so that patterns identical under reflection and rotation are counted once.

Ongoing

Active structures and embedded actuation

Getting the actuator out of the motor and into the structure itself.

Heat-shrink weavers as distributed actuators

Shrink-film strips woven directly into the sheet, so that the actuation pattern and the structural pattern are the same object. Anisotropic shrinkage turns out to matter more than it looks like it should.

Ongoing

A rotational electrostatic clutch

A clutch that engages and releases electrostatically at low power, aimed at compact transmissions where a conventional brake or motor is too heavy to carry.

Manuscript in preparation

Experimental platforms and instrumentation

Models are only worth what you can check them against. These are the rigs and pipelines that make the checking possible.

Fabrication pipeline for woven specimens

From a pattern string to a cut file to a woven sheet, so that the specimen on the bench is provably the pattern that was simulated.

Ongoing

Shape capture and characterization

Measuring the morphed geometry of a sheet well enough to compare it to a predicted surface, and measuring the load it carries once it gets there.

Ongoing

Clutch test bench

Torque, engagement time, and holding force under repeated cycling, instrumented to separate electrostatic behavior from friction.
